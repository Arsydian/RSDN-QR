RSDN-QR: free, private QR code generator

Create custom, print-ready QR codes for links, text, email, phone calls, SMS, Wi-Fi networks, contact cards (vCard), WhatsApp, crypto wallets and calendar events. Add your logo, colors and gradients, check scannability as you design, and export SVG, PNG or JPEG up to 4096px, or generate a whole batch from a CSV file.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is uploaded or stored.
